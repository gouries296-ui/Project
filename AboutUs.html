PROJECT OVERVIEW

HoneyShield is a third-year BSc Computer Science project developed in the field of Cyber Security. The project focuses on the growing threat of ransomware attacks faced by organizations and aims to provide an early warning mechanism.

Our system is designed to identify suspicious activities before they can lead to a serious security breach. HoneyShield combines monitoring and alert mechanisms to provide timely information about potential threats.

Our Team

HoneyShield is developed by a team of four BSc Computer Science students as part of our academic project. Our team combines knowledge gained through our academic coursework and additional technical courses.
